# LeetCase icon prompt

Attach `assets/voidhub-reference.png` as the visual reference, then paste:

```text
Design a professional Chrome extension icon for LeetCase, a small open-source
developer tool built by VoidHub. Use the attached VoidHub logo as the visual
family reference.

The monogram must read exactly "LS" (L then S), as requested by the owner.
Do not change it to LC or VH, and do not include the full product name.

Keep the same visual language as the reference: bold white geometric letters,
a nearly black charcoal background (#0D111B), and a single short, solid teal
underline (#2B7B87). Integrate L and S into one distinctive, balanced monogram
with clean negative space. Maintain a clear reading of both letters. It should
feel like a sibling of VoidHub, while having its own recognizable silhouette.

Flat vector-like artwork, crisp edges, restrained geometry, optical centering.
No gradients, glow, bevels, 3D effects, shadows, browser frames, mockup scenes,
extra symbols, curly braces, or additional text. Do not use LeetCode's logo.

Create one finished square 1024 × 1024 icon, using the full canvas as a solid
charcoal background. Keep the monogram and underline comfortably inside the
central 75% so nothing becomes cramped at small sizes. Prioritize legibility
at 16, 32, 48 and 128 pixels. The letter strokes must stay distinct at 16 px.
Deliver the flat icon alone, not a contact sheet or a presentation board.

If native SVG export is supported, also provide a clean editable SVG.
Otherwise deliver a high-resolution PNG without pretending it is an SVG.
```

Use the resulting design as a reference for the final extension asset. Actual
Chrome sizes and small-size legibility must be exported and checked separately.
LS is the user's chosen mark; do not silently substitute LC.
